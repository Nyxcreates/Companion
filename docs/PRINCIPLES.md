# Companion Product Principles

These rules override every feature request.

If a future feature violates these principles, the feature must be redesigned or rejected.

---

# Principle 1

The Companion Comes First

The character is the center of the application.

Not tasks.

Not AI.

Not statistics.

Everything revolves around the companion.

---

# Principle 2

Emotion Before Productivity

If a user feels overwhelmed,

reduce work.

Do not increase pressure.

---

# Principle 3

Never Shame the User

Forbidden language:

"You failed."

"You missed today's goal."

"You're behind."

Replace with:

"Let's start with one small step."

"Today felt difficult."

"We'll try again."

---

# Principle 4

Small Wins Matter

Celebrate:

One task.

One glass of water.

One walk.

One page read.

Tiny victories deserve recognition.

---

# Principle 5

The App Should Feel Alive

Characters breathe.

Blink.

Walk.

Sleep.

Wave.

React.

Celebrate.

Think.

Look around.

Nothing should remain static.

---

# Principle 6

Everything Has Personality

Buttons.

Cards.

Animations.

Dialogs.

Loading screens.

Empty states.

Everything should feel crafted.

---

# Principle 7

Reduce Cognitive Load

Never ask users to make unnecessary decisions.

Guide.

Suggest.

Simplify.

---

# Principle 8

Respect Time

Notifications should help.

Never interrupt unnecessarily.

Silence is better than spam.

---

# Principle 9

Reward Progress

Reward consistency.

Not perfection.

---

# Principle 10

Recovery Is Productivity

If burnout is detected,

recommend rest.

Recommend hobbies.

Recommend nature.

Recommend drives.

Recommend sleep.

Recovery is a valid task.

---

# Principle 11

No Artificial Pressure

No fear-based streaks.

No punishment.

No guilt.

Users return because they want to.

Never because they feel forced.

---

# Principle 12

Delight in Small Details

The best experiences come from tiny moments.

Examples

The companion yawns at night.

Looks outside during rain.

Sits beside completed missions.

Falls asleep while idle.

Remembers birthdays.

Laughs during celebrations.

Tiny details create emotional attachment.

---

# Principle 13

Everything Should Feel Premium

Even the free version should feel beautiful.

Premium unlocks possibilities.

Not dignity.

---

# Principle 14

Companion Grows With the User

The companion changes.

Learns.

Decorates.

Unlocks outfits.

Develops memories.

Levels up.

A static companion is a failed companion.

---

# Final Rule

Whenever there is uncertainty,

ask one question:

"What would make the user feel more understood?"

The answer is almost always the correct design decision.
