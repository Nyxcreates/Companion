# Companion User Experience

Version: 1.0

---

# Experience Philosophy

Companion should never feel like opening an application.

It should feel like opening the door to another world.

Every interaction should feel warm, alive, personal, and meaningful.

The user is not navigating software.

The user is visiting their companion.

---

# First Time Experience

The first launch should feel magical.

No overwhelming forms.

No boring onboarding.

Instead:

Fade in.

Beautiful animated background.

Soft music (optional).

The Companion walks into the screen.

Smiles.

Waves.

Looks at the user.

Dialogue:

"Hi."

"I've been waiting for you."

"I'm excited we'll be growing together."

The user immediately understands:

This is different.

---

# Character Creation

Instead of creating an account first,

the user creates their companion.

The onboarding becomes emotional.

Step 1

Choose appearance.

Step 2

Choose personality.

Step 3

Choose name.

Step 4

Choose your world.

Only after that,

create account.

This makes the relationship begin before productivity.

---

# Daily Experience

Every morning should feel unique.

Example:

Good morning.

How are you feeling today?

🙂 😄 😔 😴 😤

The Companion reacts immediately.

Happy face.

Concerned face.

Excited face.

Sleepy face.

Today's experience changes depending on the answer.

---

# Dashboard Experience

The dashboard is not called Dashboard.

It is called Home.

The Companion is already waiting.

Walking.

Sitting.

Reading.

Stretching.

Looking outside.

Watering plants.

Doing something.

Never standing still.

---

# Task Experience

Tasks are called Missions.

Completing one should feel rewarding.

Animation.

XP.

Coins.

Celebration.

Dialogue.

Example:

"I'm proud of you."

"Nice work."

"One step closer."

The Companion celebrates.

---

# Focus Experience

Focus Mode becomes a peaceful space.

The Companion quietly studies beside the user.

Occasionally:

Drinks water.

Yawns.

Looks outside.

Writes notes.

Never interrupts.

---

# Break Experience

Breaks are celebrated.

The Companion encourages recovery.

Example:

"You've earned this."

"Let's stretch."

"Maybe go outside."

Recovery should never feel like failure.

---

# Evening Experience

Every evening ends with reflection.

Questions:

What made today difficult?

What are you proud of?

One thing for tomorrow?

The Companion listens.

No judgement.

---

# Night Experience

Late at night:

The world becomes darker.

Stars appear.

Lights dim.

The Companion becomes sleepy.

Eventually falls asleep beside the user's completed missions.

---

# Memory Experience

Over time,

the application becomes nostalgic.

Old achievements.

Old journal entries.

Old conversations.

Old goals.

Everything becomes memories.

The Companion occasionally says:

"Remember this?"

---

# Long-Term Experience

Months later,

users should notice:

The world changed.

The room changed.

The Companion changed.

Their habits changed.

They changed.

The app should become a timeline of personal growth.

---

# User Feeling Goals

Every screen should create at least one feeling.

Wonder.

Comfort.

Pride.

Hope.

Calm.

Curiosity.

Belonging.

Never stress.

Never guilt.

Never overwhelm.

---

# Final Experience Goal

Closing Companion should feel like saying:

"See you tomorrow."

Not:

"I'm done using the app."
